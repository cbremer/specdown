## What to Test

Please update your existing SpecDown installation without deleting it first.

- Open Markdown documents from Files and iCloud Drive, then try multiple tabs and Raw/Preview. Check that a rejected or oversized file leaves the previous document and its title intact.
- Open Diagram Showcase. Expand a diagram, pan it, use zoom controls and Reset, then rotate or resize the view. The diagram should remain centered when zoom controls or view-size changes apply.
- Use Find with the keyboard open. Check next/previous matches and closing/reopening Find. Documents with many matches show up to 1,000 highlights and a `1000+` count; the remaining text must stay visible.
- Check offline Sample/Diagram Showcase, HTTPS links and images, clear errors for unavailable resources, and print/cancel.
- Confirm annotations remain unavailable in this iPhone/iPad release. Check that your existing bookmarks, theme and app preferences survive the update.

For any failure, record the device, OS, build number and steps. Fixes require a new build and affected retesting before submitting to App Review. Keep untested hardware and OS combinations explicitly untested.
