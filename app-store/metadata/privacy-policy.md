# SpecDown Privacy Policy (DRAFT — owner must confirm facts before publishing)

_Effective: [DATE]_

SpecDown is a Markdown viewer for iPhone, iPad and Mac, published by [LEGAL NAME].

**We don't collect your data.** SpecDown has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising
and no tracking. We do not operate servers that receive your documents or usage data.

**Your documents stay on your device.** Files you open are read locally to display them.
Recent files, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device.

**When the app uses the network.** SpecDown connects to the internet only when you ask
it to — for example, when you open a Markdown file from a web or GitHub link, or open a
document that includes images hosted on the web. Those requests go directly from your
device to the site that hosts the content (such as github.com), and that site's own
privacy policy applies. [MAC DIRECT-DOWNLOAD BUILD ONLY: the version downloaded from
GitHub checks GitHub for app updates. The App Store versions do not.]

**Children.** SpecDown does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
