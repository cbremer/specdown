SpecDown opens without an account. The bundled Diagram Showcase is available offline.

To try the import workflow, save the two supplied review files to Files on iPhone or iPad, or to a folder on Mac. Launch SpecDown and choose Open File.

1. Open Stargazing guide.docx. The title, section headings and paragraphs appear as Markdown.
2. Open Observing log.pdf. Its page is a scan. SpecDown recognizes the text on-device and asks you to check the transcription.
3. Choose Edit (Edit Markdown in the iPhone actions menu). Add a heading, preview the change, then use Save as Markdown to choose a destination. Reopen the saved .md file to check your edit. The original PDF remains intact.
