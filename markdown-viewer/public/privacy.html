Privacy Policy

SpecDown for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Linux and the web · Effective October 12, 2026

We don't collect your data

SpecDown has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The SpecDown developer does not operate any server that receives your documents or information about how you use the app.

Your documents stay on your device

Files you open are read on your device to display them. Recent files, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device.

When SpecDown uses the network

SpecDown connects to the internet only when you ask it to — for example, when you open a Markdown file from a web or GitHub link, or open a document that includes images hosted on the web. Those requests go directly from your device to the site that hosts the content (such as github.com), and that site's own privacy policy applies.

The web version and the desktop versions downloaded from GitHub check GitHub for new releases. The App Store versions do not; Apple delivers their updates.

Children

SpecDown does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
