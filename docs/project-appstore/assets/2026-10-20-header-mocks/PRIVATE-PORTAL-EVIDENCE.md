# Private portal evidence

Three original authenticated Apple portal screenshots are intentionally excluded from public Git history: `both-headers-uploaded-app-store-connect.png`, `device-preview-header-unassigned.png` and `halo-uploaded-app-store-connect.png`.

They are account-interface audit evidence, not the product artwork. Exact-path ignore rules preserve their original owner-local paths while preventing accidental publication. Their source hashes and reasons are listed in the [publication inventory](../../2026-11-01-publication-inventory.json). Approved artwork exports and historical source mockups remain published with their original bytes. Portal upload, review approval, assignment and final placement are distinct facts; these private captures are not current build-230 acceptance.
